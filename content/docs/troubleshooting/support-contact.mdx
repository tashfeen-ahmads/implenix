# Support Contact

Every Implenix deployment includes ongoing support. Here is how to reach us and what response times to expect.

## Standard support channels

**Deployment Slack channel.** Depending on your plan, we set up a dedicated Slack channel between your team and ours during deployment. This is the fastest channel for questions and non-urgent requests.

**Email.** support@implenix.net for general questions, feature requests, and non-urgent issues.

**Emergency phone.** A priority support number provided during deployment for active production issues. Use for agent-down scenarios, wrong routing on critical calls, or compliance emergencies.

## Response time SLAs

**Priority 1 (production outage).** Response within 30 minutes during business hours, 2 hours after hours. Resolution as fast as feasible with continuous updates.

**Priority 2 (production degradation).** Response within 2 hours during business hours, next business morning after hours.

**Priority 3 (non-urgent issue).** Response within 1 business day.

**Priority 4 (feature request or question).** Response within 3 business days.

## What information to include when reporting an issue

Faster resolution when you include:

- **Timestamp** of the issue (with time zone).
- **Call ID** if the issue is call-specific (from your dashboard).
- **Caller phone number** if applicable.
- **Description** of what happened vs what should have happened.
- **Screenshots** of any error messages or unexpected UI.
- **Impact** (how many calls affected, business impact if any).

## Business hours and after-hours coverage

**Business hours:** Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm ET.

**After-hours emergency:** 24/7 pager for Priority 1 issues. Non-emergency issues wait for the next business morning.

**Holidays:** Standard US federal holidays. Emergency support remains available.

## Escalation path

If you feel an issue is not getting appropriate attention:

1. Reply to the support ticket with "Escalation" in the subject.
2. Contact your deployment lead directly.
3. Contact the founder (Tashfeen Ahmad) via the email address provided at kickoff.

We track escalations closely; they are rare and get immediate attention.

## Change requests vs bug reports

**Bug report.** Something the agent is doing wrong (transfer failing, wrong booking, hallucinated info). Fixed as part of standard support.

**Change request.** Something we should do differently (new intent branch, updated hours, new integration, tone change). Handled through your tuning cadence, as scoped in your custom plan.

## Regular check-ins

**Weekly (first 30 days).** Post-deployment we run a 15-minute weekly check-in to review any issues and small tuning items.

**Monthly (ongoing).** 30-minute monthly deep review with QA findings, performance metrics, and any deployment adjustments.

**Quarterly.** Full deployment health review with strategic recommendations.

## Feature requests

We take feature requests seriously. Two paths:

**Deployment-specific.** Features useful for your specific deployment implemented as part of monthly tuning.

**Platform-wide.** Features that would benefit multiple customers reviewed for platform roadmap inclusion. If prioritized, implemented in an upcoming release cycle.

Submit through the standard support channel with "Feature request" in the subject.

## Feedback and improvement

We want honest feedback. If something about the service is not working for you, tell us. If something is exceeding expectations, tell us that too. Quarterly reviews are a standing time to discuss, but we welcome feedback anytime.
