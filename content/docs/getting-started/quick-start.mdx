# Quick Start

An Implenix deployment ships in 7 to 14 business days for most operations, 14 to 28 for regulated industries. This page is the compressed version of what happens in each phase.

## Phase 1, Discovery (days 1 to 2)

We listen to 20 to 40 of your recent inbound call recordings and read your existing intake script if one exists. Output: a call-category map, a caller-vocabulary catalog, and a written "hard-stop" list of things the agent should never say or do.

## Phase 2, Agent build (days 3 to 5)

We draft the 7-section prompt (system role, tone, intake script, transfer rules, escalation flows, edge cases, hard-stops). You review and approve section by section. This is the phase where operator judgment matters most.

## Phase 3, Integration wiring (days 6 to 9)

CRM connection with read and write scopes. Calendar integration with real availability read. Twilio SMS provisioning. Warm-transfer bridge configuration. Every integration verified end-to-end with a test write appearing in your source system.

## Phase 4, Test-call battery (days 10 to 12)

We run 30 to 50 mock calls against a shadow number. Standard bookings, edge cases, adversarial calls, hostile callers, unusual accents. The owner listens to every one. Anything unexpected gets tuned before cutover.

## Phase 5, Go-live (days 13 to 14)

Port your inbound number or forward it. Silent-monitor for the first 48 hours minimum, a human is available to catch a bad transfer. After 72 hours of clean operation, the deployment is production.

## What determines whether it takes 7 or 14 days

- 7 days: single-location shop, common CRM (HubSpot, Follow Up Boss, GoHighLevel), Google Calendar, unregulated industry.
- 10 to 14 days: ServiceTitan or complex ERP integration, multi-location, medspa with practice management system.
- 14 to 28 days: regulated industries (HIPAA healthcare, legal with conflict-check integration, financial services with licensing gates).
