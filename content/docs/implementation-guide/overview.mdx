# Implementation Guide Overview

An Implenix implementation is a 7 to 14 business day process (14 to 28 for regulated industries) that takes your operation from zero to a production AI voice agent. This overview page frames what happens; deeper pages cover the specifics.

## What implementation actually delivers

At the end of implementation, you have:

- An AI voice agent live on your inbound business line.
- Real-time integration with your CRM, calendar, and communication tools.
- A tuned call script matching your business vocabulary and intake needs.
- Defined escalation rules routing complex calls to your team.
- Recording and transcript archive with appropriate access controls.
- Reporting dashboard showing call volume, capture rate, and outcomes.
- Ongoing monthly tuning and QA review.

## The five phases

**Phase 1, Discovery (days 1 to 2).** We listen to your inbound calls, capture your vocabulary, and understand your operation.

**Phase 2, Build (days 3 to 5).** We draft the 7-section script and get your approval. You mark up drafts; we iterate.

**Phase 3, Integration wiring (days 6 to 9).** CRM, calendar, SMS, transfer bridge. Every integration verified end-to-end.

**Phase 4, Test (days 10 to 12).** 30 to 50 mock calls against a shadow number. You listen to every one.

**Phase 5, Go-live (days 13 to 14).** Cutover with 48+ hours silent-monitoring. Deployment enters production.

## Roles during implementation

**Your side:**

- **Deployment owner.** One person who owns the deployment. Joins discovery, reviews scripts, signs off on go-live.
- **Ops lead or office manager.** Provides operational context, reviews escalation rules.
- **IT contact.** Provides API credentials, sets up call forwarding, coordinates any telecom changes.
- **Team members.** Available for consultation on specific script sections (e.g., billing questions for the billing team lead).

**Our side:**

- **Deployment lead.** Your primary contact throughout implementation.
- **Integrations engineer.** Handles CRM, calendar, and telephony wiring.
- **Prompt engineer.** Drafts and iterates the call script.
- **QA lead.** Runs the test-call battery.
- **Founder involvement.** Available for escalation on scope, compliance, or strategic questions.

## What accelerates vs slows implementation

**Accelerates:**
- Clear ownership on your side.
- Available API credentials from the start.
- Existing call recordings we can review.
- Fast turnaround on script reviews (under 2 business days).
- Willingness to test during business-hour windows.

**Slows:**
- Multiple stakeholders needing to sign off on every decision.
- Delayed API access.
- Long review cycles on scripts (a week or more per iteration).
- Custom integration requirements that emerge mid-deployment.
- Regulatory review needed after initial scope was set.

## Post-implementation

After go-live, deployments enter monthly tuning cadence. Standard package includes:

- Monthly QA review of 20 to 30 flagged calls.
- Script adjustments based on review findings.
- Monthly performance reporting.
- Quarterly deep-review of deployment health.
- Ongoing support for issues or expansion.
