# Number Provisioning

If you want a new number for your Implenix agent rather than porting or forwarding an existing one, we provision it through Twilio in about 10 minutes. Here are your options and the trade-offs.

## Number types

**Local numbers.** A local area code number in a city or region you specify. Best for local-service businesses where callers recognize the area code. Available in most US and Canadian cities.

**Toll-free numbers.** 800, 833, 844, 855, 866, 877, 888 prefixes. Best for national-facing businesses. Slightly higher monthly cost per number.

**Vanity numbers.** Custom digit patterns like 800-555-CARE or 800-BUY-NOW. Available but with limited inventory. Additional lookup fee. Not usually worth the extra cost unless the vanity is a genuine brand asset.

## What you configure at provisioning

- Area code preference (for local numbers).
- Voice-enabled: yes.
- SMS-enabled: recommended for confirmation messages and follow-up sequences.
- MMS-enabled: optional, needed only for media messages.
- Emergency (911) address if the number will be used for outbound.
- A2P 10DLC registration (for SMS from local numbers, required by carriers).

## A2P 10DLC brief overview

If your deployment uses SMS from a local number to consumer mobiles, you need A2P 10DLC brand and campaign registration. This is a carrier requirement, not an Implenix preference. Registration takes 1 to 5 business days and requires a business EIN and a use-case description. We handle the paperwork; you provide the business info. Registration runs in parallel with setup: your voice agent still goes live in 72 hours, and SMS from the local number switches on as soon as the carrier approves the campaign.

Toll-free numbers do not require A2P 10DLC but have their own compliance layer.

## Numbers per location

Multi-location deployments typically use one number per location so caller ID matches local expectations, especially useful for outbound. Alternatively, a single national number with location-aware routing works if the caller identity is not tied to a specific location.

## Cost

Twilio local numbers run $1.15 per month per number. Toll-free numbers run $2.00 per month. Voice minutes and SMS are billed per-usage on top. For a standard small-business deployment, telephony costs are a small fraction of the monthly agent bill.

## Porting existing numbers instead

If you want to keep your existing published number, we port it to Twilio rather than provisioning a new one. Porting is a carrier process that takes 5 to 10 business days and requires signed Letter of Authorization. It does not hold up go-live: we forward your existing number to the agent so you are live in 72 hours, and the port runs in parallel until it completes.
