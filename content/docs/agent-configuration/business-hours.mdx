# Business Hours

The Implenix agent runs 24/7 by default, but many deployments want different behavior during and outside business hours. Here is how to configure the schedule.

## Business hours model

**Standard hours.** Days of the week and time ranges when your business is normally open. Standard example: Monday to Friday 8am to 6pm, Saturday 9am to 2pm, closed Sunday.

**Extended hours.** Sometimes an operation runs different schedules for different services (dispatch line 24/7, front office 9 to 5). Each schedule is configured independently.

**Holidays.** Federal holidays plus any business-specific closures. The agent respects these automatically and applies after-hours behavior on holiday dates.

## What changes at business-hour transitions

**Greeting.** The agent can use different greetings during and outside hours. Example: "Good morning, thanks for calling. How can I help?" during hours vs "This is the after-hours line, how can I help?" outside hours.

**Escalation targets.** Different on-call rotations for after-hours vs business-hours calls. Emergency triage routes to the on-call tech at 3am, to the front desk at 2pm.

**Available services.** Some services only book during business hours (like a dental cleaning that requires a hygienist available). Others (like emergency dispatch) run 24/7. The agent respects these constraints.

**Tone.** Some deployments use slightly warmer tone during business hours and more efficient / direct tone after hours.

## Time zone handling

Every schedule is defined in your business's local time zone. Callers from other time zones see your schedule, not theirs. If your business spans multiple time zones (multi-location), each location's schedule uses its own time zone.

## Overriding hours for specific days

- **Extended hours for special events.** Configure a one-time override for a specific date.
- **Early closures.** Same mechanism, closes at a specific time on a specific date.
- **Emergency closures.** For unforeseen closures (weather, power outage), we can push an emergency override same-day. Contact support.

## What happens outside business hours

Depending on your configuration:

**Full agent handling.** Agent answers and handles the call the same way it would during business hours. Standard for 24/7 operations.

**Emergency-only handling.** Agent answers, triages, and handles only emergency intake. Non-emergency callers are told hours and offered to schedule a callback.

**Message-taking mode.** Agent answers, takes a message, promises callback in a specific window. Suitable for lower-volume operations.

**Complete forward to voicemail.** Rare, but supported if you want AI answering only during specific windows.

## Configuring hours

Business hours are configured during discovery. Post-deployment changes require a support ticket and take 24 to 48 hours to apply, primarily because we test the change against your active flows before pushing to production.
