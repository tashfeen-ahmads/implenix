## The before state

GTR Improvements runs three trade categories in parallel, roofing,
kitchen, and bath remodels, with average project values around
**$24,500** across the mix. The team is two project managers, an
8-person crew, and Lexi the owner running scheduling, sales, and
billing.

The structural problem: every job has three communication phases
(initial inquiry → assessment → execution coordination → quality
check), and inbound urgent calls collide with all of them. A roof
leak at 11am stops the assessment scheduling that was supposed to
happen in the next 30 minutes. The kitchen install team needs a
materials decision from the homeowner, and the call goes unanswered
because Lexi is on a roof at the moment.

Pre-deployment baseline:

- **Inbound calls/month:** 286
- **Calls answered live within 2 minutes:** 49%
- **Average project inquiry response time:** 3h 40min
- **Quote-to-close rate on answered inquiries:** 27%
- **No-show rate on scheduled assessments:** 18%

Lexi during discovery: *"Urgent calls, comms with the building team,
in-house assessments, then quality checks on roof, kitchen, and bath
the day is gone before I look up."*

## What we shipped

A multi-trade deployment with five work streams:

1. **Emergency triage for urgent calls.** Keywords like "leak,"
   "water," "active damage," "roof off," route to the on-call PM via
   SIP transfer within 8 seconds. Other urgent calls (no-water, no-heat) follow secondary escalation paths.
2. **Structured project intake.** Project type, square footage, scope
   summary, timeline, budget posture, decision-maker, insurance
   involvement, photos requested via SMS link. Captured to Jobber
   during the call.
3. **Assessment booking against the field calendar.** Books the
   appropriate PM against their real availability, accounting for
   drive time between job sites.
4. **Crew coordination handoff.** When the AI flags a call as a
   materials/scope decision from an active job's homeowner, it routes
   directly to the PM running that job with a context briefing.
5. **Post-install quality-check sequence.** 48-hour SMS check-in,
   7-day voice follow-up, 30-day satisfaction survey. Runs on every
   completed project.

Parallel-run for 7 days before cutover.

## What we learned

Multi-trade contractors are the hardest deployment we have shipped
because the call profile is wider than any single-trade business. The
key insight: route by trade BEFORE you route by urgency. A roof
emergency goes to the roofing PM, a kitchen install scope question
goes to the kitchen PM, and the AI determines this in the first 15
seconds of the call. Without that trade-first routing, urgent calls
end up at the wrong PM and the value proposition collapses.

If your operation spans multiple trades and the owner is acting as
dispatcher, the GTR shape replicates well.
