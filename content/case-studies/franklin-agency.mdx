## The before state

Franklin Agency runs a 12-person team serving ~22 active client
accounts, performance marketing, paid acquisition, and conversion
optimization. Average client retainer runs **$4,200/month**, average
client tenure about 14 months.

The structural problem: the agency's own inbound was eating the time
the team was supposed to spend on client work. Discovery calls,
partner inquiries, vendor questions, and sales-prospect calls were
landing on the partners, and on the senior strategists when partners
were unavailable.

Franklin's discovery framing was tight: *"Our agency's main goal is to
get clients' work done so they grow with more leads. But we are also
tackling our own inbound chapter, and it eats time we cannot afford
to lose."*

Pre-deployment baseline:

- **Inbound calls/month:** 152 across discovery, partner, vendor mix
- **Calls answered live within 5 minutes:** 38%
- **Discovery-call booking rate from inbound:** 22%
- **Partner discovery-call calendar capacity used:** ~95% during peak
  weeks (no buffer)
- **Tracked weekly hours on agency-internal phone work:** 38 hrs across
  team

## What we shipped

An agency-tuned deployment with five work streams:

1. **Structured prospect qualification.** Budget tier (sub-$3K,
   $3-7K, $7K+), timeline (immediate, 30-day, 60-day, exploratory),
   current funnel state (cold, warm-traffic, paid running), industry
   ICP check (we are best for X, Y, Z verticals). Captures all to
   HubSpot.
2. **Discovery-call booking against partner calendars.** Books only
   qualified prospects who pass the ICP and budget threshold. Sub-threshold prospects route into a self-serve resource sequence
   instead.
3. **Partner/vendor triage.** Existing partner calls route to the
   relevant account lead via SMS. Vendor inquiries route to ops.
   Cold-prospect partner inquiries get the qualification flow.
4. **4-touch follow-up sequence on qualified prospects.** Day 0
   confirmation, Day 2 SMS check-in, Day 5 voice follow-up, Day 14
   final touch with a piece of relevant content.
5. **Hot-lead Slack alerts.** Sub-7-day timeline + $7K+ budget +
   confirmed ICP fit pings the founders' Slack channel immediately,
   even outside business hours.

Parallel-run for 4 days before cutover.

## What we learned

Agencies have a counterintuitive ROI profile: the dollar value is
*not* in the new clients signed (although there were two of them).
The dollar value is in the *retention* of existing clients, because
client work delivered per week is directly proportional to the team's
focused attention. The AI's main job at Franklin is not "answer more
prospects", it is "give the team back the hours that were leaking out
to phone work."

If your agency runs 10-30 active client accounts and the partners are
running discovery calls, the Franklin shape is likely close to your
operation.
