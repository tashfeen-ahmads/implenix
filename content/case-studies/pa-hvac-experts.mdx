## The before state

PA HVAC Experts was running a familiar five-truck operation: one front-desk
operator handling daytime inbound, calls rolling to the owner's mobile
after hours, and a Google Sheet for follow-ups that was honored when
someone remembered. Average job value was running around **$1,150**, mix of $650 residential service calls and $8,000-$15,000 install jobs.

Carrier-side records pulled during onboarding showed the real picture
the team did not have visibility into: of **412 inbound calls in the
prior 30 days, 174 had abandoned mid-ring or hit voicemail**. That is a
**42% miss rate**, and at a 38% close rate × $1,150 average value,
roughly **$76,000/month in lost pipeline**.

Alex's own estimate before we ran the audit: "maybe 20% miss rate."

## What we shipped

Five-tier scope:

1. **24/7 inbound coverage.** The AI picks up before voicemail engages,
   triages emergency vs scheduled, captures the intake script Alex's
   front desk had drifted on, and books against the live ServiceTitan
   dispatch calendar.
2. **Emergency triage rules.** Keywords like "no heat," "no AC,"
   "flooded," "smell gas," and "carbon monoxide" route to the on-call
   tech via SIP transfer within 6 seconds.
3. **CRM enrichment on every call.** Address, symptoms, system age,
   warranty status, payment method, and decision-maker all logged to
   ServiceTitan during the call.
4. **4-touch follow-up sequence.** Day 0 confirmation, Day 1 SMS check-in, Day 3 voice follow-up, Day 7 final touch. Runs on every quote
   automatically.
5. **Live transfer rules.** Long-time commercial accounts, calls over
   a $5K commercial threshold, and any caller asking for Alex by name
   route to him directly with a context briefing.

Deployment ran 11 business days. Parallel-run with the existing front
desk for 6 days before cutover.

## What we learned

The HVAC playbook has held up cleanly across 15+ deployments now, but
PA HVAC validated two things specifically:

1. **Emergency triage rules are the highest-leverage piece.** The
   storm-driven surge weeks alone produced enough captured-pipeline
   value to pay for 6 months of the AI.
2. **Follow-up automation is the silent compounder.** Alex's quote-to-close rate moved from 22% to 31% just from the 4-touch sequence
   running consistently, independent of any improvement to the call
   handling itself.

If your HVAC operation matches the shape of PA HVAC Experts, 3-10
trucks, 1-2 front-desk, owner pulled into evening dispatch, the
deployment plays out roughly the same way.
