## The before state

Parnell Motors is a single-location used-and-pre-owned dealer with six
sales reps and one BDC manager. Average sold-vehicle value runs about
**$22,400** across the lot mix, heavier on the $18-28K range with a
small premium pre-owned segment above $40K.

The bottleneck Parnell described in discovery: *"Phones pull the team
off the floor."* The math runs cleanly, a sales rep on the phone for
20 minutes is not the rep walking the showroom customer to a vehicle.
The BDC manager was supposed to absorb inbound, but volume kept
exceeding her capacity, especially during evening and weekend peaks.

Pre-deployment baseline:

- **Inbound calls/month:** 540 across all numbers
- **Calls answered live within 60 seconds:** 47%
- **Average inbound-to-test-drive conversion:** 16%
- **Median follow-up lag on quoted vehicles:** 27 hours
- **Test drives booked from inbound:** 87/month

Parnell's framing was characteristically short: *"I appreciate
communication and organization."* The phone operation was neither.

## What we shipped

A dealer-tuned deployment with four work streams:

1. **Structured buyer intake.** Vehicle of interest (year/make/model
   or category), trade-in details (year/make/model/mileage/condition),
   financing preference (cash, finance, lease, in-house), budget
   range, timeline, contact preference. All logged to DealerSocket
   during the call.
2. **Live test-drive booking.** Books against the floor schedule
   honoring each rep's calendar and the lot's general-floor coverage
   needs. Texts the buyer a confirmation with vehicle details and rep
   name.
3. **5-touch follow-up sequence.** Day 0 SMS confirmation, Day 1 voice
   follow-up, Day 4 SMS check-in, Day 10 voice re-engagement, Day 21
   final touch. Runs on every quoted vehicle.
4. **Hot-lead escalation.** Cash-buyer + sub-7-day timeline routes to
   the sales manager via SMS in under 90 seconds with a context
   briefing. The manager's close rate on these escalated leads runs
   well above general BDC conversion.

Parallel-run for 5 days before cutover.

  the phone.

## What we learned

Dealer BDC has a specific failure mode: rep-on-the-phone is a measurable
sales loss because the alternative is rep-on-the-floor. The Implenix
deployment value here is not "answer more calls", it is "stop pulling
reps off the showroom." The 29% test-drive lift is partly volume
recovery, but the second-order showroom-conversion lift is where the
real dollar value lands.
