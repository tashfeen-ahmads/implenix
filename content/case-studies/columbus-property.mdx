## The before state

Columbus Property is a four-person team running a real-estate
acquisitions operation: paid Google traffic driving inbound seller
calls, two closers running acquisitions conversations, two support
staff handling the rest of the operation. Average deal value runs
around **$11,500** on the assignment side, **$28,000** on the
flip side.

The bottleneck the team felt every day: calls came in faster than the
people who could close them could pick up. Closers were either on a
call (good, closing) or running their pipeline (good, follow-up), and
new inbound leads were waiting 6-8 hours for first contact. Most
sellers in motivated-distressed situations do not wait 6-8 hours.

The pre-deployment numbers from the carrier records and Follow Up Boss:

- **Inbound calls/month:** 178
- **Calls answered live within 5 minutes:** 41%
- **First-touch response time (median):** 6h 12min
- **Lead-to-appointment rate:** 14%
- **Appointment-to-deal rate:** 23%

Yessy's framing during the discovery call: *"We juggle deals,
appointments, and follow-ups all day, but we're a 4-person team and
something has to give."*

## What we shipped

Two-part scope:

1. **Inbound qualification + booking.** The AI answers every call,
   captures the property address, the seller's situation (foreclosure,
   probate, tired landlord, relocation, etc.), condition snapshot,
   target number, and timeline. Books a 15-minute acquisitions
   conversation against the closer's real calendar if the lead is
   qualified. Hot leads (motivated + sub-90-day) escalate to a closer
   via SMS within 60 seconds.
2. **5-touch nurture sequence.** Callers who hang up before booking,
   or whose timeline is 3+ months out, drop into a nurture sequence:
   Day 0 SMS confirmation, Day 1 voice follow-up, Day 4 SMS check-in,
   Day 14 voice re-engagement, Day 30 final touch. Runs automatically.

Live in 13 business days. Parallel-run for 7 days before cutover.

The closers' calendars now stay full of *qualified* acquisitions calls
instead of full of triage. The 11-percentage-point lift on the lead-to-appointment rate is doing most of the dollar work, combined with the
appointment-to-deal rate holding steady at 23%, the closed-deal volume
moved up roughly 28% over the 90-day window.

Specific deltas:

- **Median first-touch response time:** 6h 12min → 47 seconds.
- **Appointments booked per week:** 6 → 11.
- **Closer time spent on qualification:** 90 min/day → ~10 min/day.
- **Recovered after-hours pipeline:** 22 leads in the first 60 days
  that came in outside business hours and got booked before the team
  was even awake.

## What we learned

Real-estate acquisitions has the cleanest hot-lead escalation rule we
have shipped: *motivated seller × sub-90-day timeline* is a binary
match, and the SMS-to-closer escalation lands well inside the first-contact window that drives close rate. Where the AI handles the rest
of the funnel, closers' time concentrates entirely on the 25-30% of
calls that have real deal potential.

If your operation looks like Columbus Property's, paid traffic,
closers limited by attention, follow-up running on memory, the same
shape of deployment usually produces a similar lift inside 90 days.
