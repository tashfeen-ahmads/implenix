## Gemini app and Gemini Agent (consumer)

Gemini is Google's consumer AI assistant, competing with ChatGPT
and Claude. It reasons across text, images, audio, and video, and
connects to Google services like Calendar, Maps, and Gmail.

Gemini Agent is the mode that takes actions for you: browsing
sites, filling in forms, and working through multi-step tasks.
It absorbed Google's earlier browser-agent experiment, Project
Mariner, which Google shut down in May 2026 and folded into
Gemini Agent and AI Mode in Search. Deep Research, which reads
and summarizes dozens of sources into a report, is another
agent-style feature in the same app.

Use case: personal and individual productivity. Not built to
answer a business phone line.

## The Gemini agent for work (announced October 2026)

At its Gemini at Work event on October 8, 2026, Google Cloud
[announced the Gemini agent](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/gemini-at-work/),
a single agent for enterprise work that answers questions, runs
tasks, creates content, and writes code. Reported details include:

- Running long jobs persistently in the cloud, so work continues
  after you close your laptop.
- Connecting to business systems such as Salesforce, ServiceNow,
  and Jira, plus any MCP server.
- Routing each job to the model that fits it, including
  non-Google models.
- Governance controls such as agent identity, audit trails, and
  spending caps.

At announcement, Google had not published general availability
dates or pricing, and said industry-specific agents for areas
like healthcare, legal, and retail would follow. It is an
enterprise knowledge-work agent, not a phone agent.

If "connects to any MCP server" sounds like jargon, I explain MCP
in plain English in

## Gemini in Google Workspace

Gemini is built into Workspace apps:

- **Gmail:** draft replies, summarize threads, pull out action
  items.
- **Docs:** draft, summarize, translate, rewrite.
- **Meet:** notes, summaries, and action items from meetings.
- **Sheets:** natural-language questions, formulas, cleanup.
- **Slides:** generate drafts and images from prompts.

Use case: productivity for organizations already on Workspace.
Real value for teams that live in Google's suite. Not a
phone-answering solution.

## Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform (formerly Vertex AI)

This is Google Cloud's platform for developers who want to build
their own agents. Google announced it at Cloud Next in April 2026
as the successor to Vertex AI, pulling Vertex AI Agent Builder,
the Agent Development Kit (ADK), Agent Engine (the managed
runtime), and model access into one product. You will still see
"Vertex AI Agent Builder" in older articles and docs.

What you get:
- Access to Gemini and many third-party models.
- Code-first (ADK) and low-code ways to build agents.
- Managed deployment, memory, and scaling.
- Enterprise controls: identity, access management, audit logs.

Use case: engineering teams building custom agent applications.
It is a set of building blocks, not a finished product for an
operator.

## Customer Engagement Suite (contact-center AI)

This is the closest Google gets to a phone agent. Google Cloud's
Customer Engagement Suite bundles Conversational Agents (virtual
agents for self-service on voice and chat), Agent Assist (real-time
help for human reps), conversation analytics, and a cloud contact
center. It integrates with contact-center platforms such as
Genesys, NICE, Five9, Cisco, and Twilio.

It is built for large contact centers with an IT team or a
systems integrator. A local plumber or a two-location dental
practice is not who this is designed for, and it will not show
up configured for your intake questions, your scheduling software,
and your transfer rules.

## Google's AI already calls businesses: Duplex to AI calling in Search

Google demoed Duplex at I/O in 2018: an assistant that phoned a
hair salon and a restaurant to book on a user's behalf. It
launched narrowly on Pixel phones and later faded into other
features.

The idea is back. Google Search now has AI that phones local
businesses for you: search for something like a pet groomer, ask
Google to check pricing, and its AI
[calls nearby businesses](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-search-can-now-call-local-businesses-using-ai/551294/)
to ask about prices and availability, then sends you a summary.
Google has said businesses are chosen using its normal local
rankings and that businesses can opt out.

This is the part most operators miss. The most important Google
AI agent for a local business may be the one calling your front
desk, not one you buy. If that call rings out or hits a voicemail
box, you are simply left out of the summary the customer reads.
Answering every call, including the ones from a machine, is now
part of local search. That is exactly the job of an
[AI receptionist](/), and it is one reason
[callers who hang up before reaching anyone](/blog/why-callers-hang-up-before-reaching-anyone)
are getting more expensive.

## Where Google fits, and where it does not

### Google fits well

- **Workspace productivity.** If your team lives in Workspace,
  Gemini there is a natural addition.
- **Custom agents on Google Cloud.** The Gemini Enterprise Agent
  Platform is a serious place to build if you have engineers.
- **Enterprise contact centers.** The Customer Engagement Suite is
  a real option at that scale.
- **Personal assistance.** Gemini and Gemini Agent compete
  directly with ChatGPT and Claude.

### Google does not fit

- **An AI receptionist for a small business phone line.** There
  is no turnkey Google product for this. Businesses here use
  specialized vendors, Implenix among them.
- **Vertical-specific voice AI** (HVAC dispatch, dental intake,
  legal intake). Google's platforms are horizontal; the vertical
  scripts, integrations, and transfer rules have to be built on
  top. That is the work we do for
  [HVAC companies](/ai-receptionist-for-hvac-companies),
  [dental practices](/ai-receptionist-for-dentists), and
  [law firms](/ai-receptionist-for-law-firms).
- **Done-for-you compliance scoping.** HIPAA, call-recording
  consent, and TCPA rules for outbound calls all need deployment
  decisions that a horizontal platform will not make for you.

## Google's models power many non-Google agents

Even though Google does not sell an AI receptionist, Gemini models
run under the hood of many voice AI products. Vendors combine a
model (Gemini, GPT, Claude, or open-source) with telephony,
speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and orchestration to build the
actual phone agent. So "does Google have an AI agent" is a
different question from "am I using Google technology." You may be
using Google's models through a non-Google vendor.

Google also launched the open Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol in 2025
so agents from different vendors can work together, which I cover
in [can AI agents talk to each other](/blog/multi-agent-systems-crypto-agi).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Gemini an AI agent?**
Gemini is a family of models and an assistant app. In plain chat
it is an assistant. In Gemini Agent mode, or when developers give
it tools, it acts as an agent: it plans steps and takes actions.

**What is the name of Google's AI agent?**
There is no single one. For consumers it is Gemini Agent in the
Gemini app. For businesses it is the Gemini agent announced in
October 2026, plus agents built on the Gemini Enterprise Agent
Platform.

**Does Google have a free AI agent?**
The Gemini app has a free tier, and Google's AI calling in Search
has been free to searchers with higher limits for paid
subscribers. The more capable agent features sit in paid Google
AI and Workspace plans.

**Can I use Gemini to build my own voice agent?**
Yes, technically. You would combine Gemini with a telephony
provider, speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and orchestration logic.
That is what many voice AI platforms do under the hood. The hard
part is not the model; it is the call flow, integrations, and
edge cases.

**What happened to Vertex AI Agent Builder?**
Google folded it into the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform,
announced in April 2026. The building blocks (ADK, Agent Engine)
carried over.

**Can I stop Google's AI from calling my business?**
Google has said businesses can opt out of its AI calls. Before you
do, consider that those calls come from people actively comparing
prices. Answering them well is usually worth more than blocking
them.

**What is the difference between Gemini and Google Assistant?**
Google Assistant was the earlier voice assistant. Gemini is its
more capable successor, and Google has been replacing Assistant
with Gemini across its devices.
