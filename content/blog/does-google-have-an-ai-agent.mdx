<Answer q="Does Google have an AI agent?">
Yes, Google has multiple AI agent products in 2026: Gemini (their consumer-facing AI assistant that reasons across text, image, video, and audio), Gemini for Google Workspace (embedded in Gmail, Docs, Meet, Sheets), Vertex AI Agent Builder (their platform for developers to build custom agents), and Duplex (voice agent technology that has been rolled into other products). Each serves a different use case. For business phone deployments specifically, the AI receptionist / voice-agent-on-inbound category, Google does not offer a turnkey product; that space is served by specialized vendors on Google Cloud infrastructure or on other clouds.
</Answer>

"Does Google have an AI agent" is a question I hear from
operators trying to figure out whether they should look at
Google's offerings before working with specialized vendors.
The honest answer is that Google has several agent products, none of them are exactly the AI receptionist category that
most small businesses are shopping for, but understanding
what Google does offer helps orient the decision.

## The current Google AI agent lineup

### Gemini (consumer)

Google's primary consumer AI assistant, competing with ChatGPT
and Claude. Available as a mobile app, web interface, and
integrated into Google Search results.

Capabilities: multi-modal reasoning (text, images, video,
audio), web browsing, integration with Google services
(Calendar, Maps, YouTube). Can accomplish multi-step tasks
within the conversation.

Use case: general-purpose AI assistant for individuals. Not
built for business phone deployments.

### Gemini for Google Workspace

Gemini embedded across Google Workspace products.

- **Gmail:** draft responses, summarize threads, extract
  action items.
- **Docs:** draft documents, summarize content, translate,
  rewrite.
- **Meet:** real-time transcription, meeting summaries, action
  item extraction.
- **Sheets:** natural language queries, formula generation,
  data cleanup.
- **Slides:** generate presentations from prompts, image
  generation.

Use case: workplace productivity for organizations already on
Google Workspace. Substantial value for teams that use Google's
suite heavily. Not a phone-answering solution.

### Vertex AI Agent Builder

Google Cloud's platform for developers to build custom AI
agents.

- Model access to Gemini and other models via API.
- Agent frameworks and tooling (Agent Development Kit).
- Tool integration through function calling.
- Deployment infrastructure on Google Cloud.
- Enterprise features (VPC, IAM, audit logging).

Use case: enterprises and development teams building custom
agent applications. Not a turnkey product for end operators.

### Duplex and voice AI

Google Duplex was the original 2018 voice agent that made
appointment-booking phone calls on behalf of users. Duplex's
underlying technology has been integrated into other Google
products (call screening on Pixel phones, Google Assistant
features).

Google does not sell Duplex as a business-facing AI
receptionist product. The technology exists inside Google;
the productized version for business phone use does not.

### Google Business Profile "Call History"

Google Business Profile offers call tracking and some AI-assisted call features (screening, message capture) for small
businesses. Useful for basic call awareness, not for full
call-handling automation.

## Where Google fits in the AI agent landscape

For business operators evaluating AI agents, here is the
practical breakdown of where Google's offerings fit:

### Fits Google well

- **Workspace productivity augmentation.** If your team lives
  in Google Workspace, Gemini for Workspace is a natural
  addition and delivers measurable time savings.
- **Custom agent development on Google Cloud infrastructure.**
  Vertex AI Agent Builder is a serious enterprise platform
  for building custom agent applications.
- **General-purpose consumer AI assistance.** Gemini competes
  with ChatGPT and Claude in this space.

### Does NOT fit Google

- **AI receptionist for a small business phone line.** Google
  does not offer a turnkey product for this. Businesses in
  this category use specialized vendors (Implenix, Retell-based platforms, Vapi-based platforms, or similar).
- **Vertical-specific voice AI** (HVAC dispatch, dental
  intake, legal intake). Google's platforms are horizontal;
  vertical solutions require additional building.
- **Turnkey compliance-scoped deployments.** HIPAA, TCPA, or
  legal-industry compliant voice agents require specialized
  scoping that Google's platforms do not deliver out of the
  box.

## The technical relationship: Google's models power many
non-Google agents

Even though Google does not sell an AI receptionist directly,
Google's Gemini models are used under the hood by many voice
AI vendors. Vendors compose Gemini (or other models, OpenAI's
GPT, Anthropic's Claude, open-source models) with telephony,
speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and orchestration layers to
build the actual voice agent products.

So the question "does Google have an AI agent" is not the same
as "am I using Google technology in my AI agent." You may
absolutely be using Google's models even when working with a
non-Google vendor.

## How to evaluate Google's offerings for your business

- **Are you already on Google Workspace?** If yes, Gemini for
  Workspace is easy to adopt and delivers measurable value.
- **Are you building a custom agent application?** Vertex AI
  Agent Builder is a serious platform. So are AWS Bedrock
  Agents and Azure AI. Evaluate all three.
- **Do you need a phone-answering AI for your business?**
  Google is not the answer here. Look at specialized voice AI
  vendors.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Gemini an AI agent?**
Gemini is a large language model with some agent-like
capabilities. When used as a chatbot, it is not an agent by
strict definition. When integrated with tools that let it take
actions, it becomes one.

**Can I use Gemini API to build my own voice agent?**
Yes, technically. You would combine Gemini with a telephony
provider (Twilio, Vonage), a speech-to-text service, a text-to-speech service, and orchestration logic. That is what many
voice AI platforms do under the hood.

**Does Google have a HIPAA-compliant AI agent?**
Google Workspace can be configured for HIPAA compliance with a
signed BAA. Vertex AI has HIPAA-eligible services. For a
custom-built healthcare voice agent, HIPAA compliance is
possible but requires careful architecture.

**What is the difference between Gemini and Google Assistant?**
Google Assistant was Google's earlier consumer voice assistant.
Gemini has been positioned as its more capable successor,
though Google Assistant continues to exist in certain contexts.

**Is Google's AI better than OpenAI's or Anthropic's?**
Benchmark comparisons vary by task and evolve monthly. For
production deployments, the practical difference between
top-tier models is usually smaller than the deployment
architecture around them.

## Where to go from here

If you are evaluating AI for productivity work within your
company (email, docs, meetings), start with Gemini for
Workspace if you use Google. If you are looking to build
custom agents on cloud infrastructure, evaluate Vertex AI
Agent Builder alongside AWS Bedrock and Azure AI. If you are
looking for an AI receptionist for a small business phone
line, Google does not have a direct answer, look at
specialized voice AI vendors.
